The app3 questions
What is this app for?

When you open an app you flagged because of an ethical problem it causes (data resale, addictive design, exploitation of workers…), a short screen asks whether you really want to open it.

It forbids nothing: it only adds a moment of reflection, so that using the app is a choice rather than a reflex.

How do I flag an app?

In the Apps tab, tap an app and tick the issues that concern you. You can also start from an issue, in the Issues tab, and link several apps to it. If an issue is missing, you can add your own.

What do the buttons on the interruption screen do?

“No, close the app” takes you back to the home screen.

“Yes, open the app” opens it, and you are not asked again for 15 minutes after you last used it, even if you close the app by mistake and reopen it. The choice is forgotten earlier if the screen turns off.

The “Don’t ask again for…” link pauses the interruption for that app, for the duration set in Settings.

Privacy3 questions
Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your phone: the apps you flagged, your issues, your settings and your statistics. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the app does not even request the Internet permission.

Why does the app need the accessibility service?

It is the only way Android lets an app know which app has just come to the front, so that the interruption can be shown at the right moment.

The service does not read what is on your screen or what you type: it only sees the name of the app that opens. You can turn it off at any time in Android’s settings.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the app removes everything. You can also clear its storage from Android’s settings, in the app’s information page.

Ethics2 questions
Who decides which apps are problematic?

You do. The app gives no verdict and keeps no list of “bad” apps: you choose, among a list of issues or your own, the ones that matter to you.

Is this free software?

Yes. The app is published under the GPL v3 licence, with no advertising and no tracking.

Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: the app keeps everything on your phone and sends nothing anywhere.

What the app stores

On your phone only: the apps you flagged, your issues and favorites, your settings, and statistics about your own choices (how many times the pause appeared and what you answered). None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and none of it is included in Android's cloud backups.

What the app does not do

  • It has no Internet permission: it cannot send data over the network.
  • It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.
  • It does not read what is on your screen, what you type or your messages.

The accessibility service

To show the pause at the right moment, the app uses Android's accessibility service. It only detects the name of the app that comes to the foreground. It does not read the content of the screen, and the name is used only on your phone to decide whether to show the pause. You can turn the service off at any time in Android's settings.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything. You can also clear its storage from Android's settings.
